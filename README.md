## 🛠️ Skills

Most of my development work revolves around Minecraft Bedrock, creating server plugins with PowerNukkitX and extending proxy functionality through WaterdogPE. Java is my main language, with JavaScript and PHP rounding out my toolkit. 

For data storage, I’ve worked with MariaDB and SQL and have some familiarity with MongoDB. My day-to-day workflow is built around IntelliJ IDEA and WebStorm, with Git and GitHub keeping projects organized and changes easy to track.

---

## 💻 About Me

I’ve spent the past 11 years turning ideas into things people can play, use, and enjoy. As a full-stack developer, I’m especially drawn to Minecraft projects and indie games, where the technical side and the player experience go hand in hand. Depending on the project, I work with Java, JavaScript, PHP, C#, C++, Ruby, or Lua, backed by MariaDB or MongoDB. 

Design has been part of that journey from the start, too—from UI/UX and graphics to animation and motion design. JetBrains IDEs and Adobe’s creative tools are usually somewhere in the mix, but what matters most to me is how everything comes together: reliable code, clean visuals, and details that make playing feel good. Outside of Minecraft, I’m putting that same care into bringing my own indie game ideas to life.
